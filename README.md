<h1>Traditional Sarees of India</h1>
<p>A simple website showcasing traditional sarees from different states of India.</p>

<h3>Project Overview</h3>
<p>This project explores the unique styles and designs of Indian sarees. It is built to practice front-end design skills.</p>

<h3>Key Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Interactive India map on the homepage</li>
  <li>Sidebar for easy navigation</li>
  <li>Pages for different saree types</li>
  <li>Image galleries and product cards</li>
  <li>Sample seller form</li>
</ul>

<h3>Technologies Used</h3>
<p>HTML and CSS</p>
