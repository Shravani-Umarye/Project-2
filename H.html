Banarasi Sarees of Uttarpradesh

Banarasi sarees are luxurious, handwoven silk sarees originating from the holy city of Varanasi (Banaras) in Uttar Pradesh, India, a region historically known for its silk trade and skilled weavers. They are characterized by their use of intricate gold and silver zari (metallic threads), complex traditional motifs like floral patterns and Mughal-inspired designs, and high-quality silk fabric. These renowned sarees are a symbol of Indian heritage and are a favorite choice for weddings and other celebrations due to their elegance and craftsmanship.
